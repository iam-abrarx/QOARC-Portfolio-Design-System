02 — Problem & Approach

Support triage ate 40 hours a week.

Every inbound support ticket was read, categorized, and routed by hand before anyone could work on it. At volume, that triage step alone consumed a full-time role's worth of hours every week — and the backlog grew faster than the team could read it.

The fix isn't "add AI to support" as a headline. It's a classification model narrow enough to trust, wired into the queue at the one step that was actually the bottleneck.

01

Classify

Every ticket is scored against the existing category taxonomy the moment it lands — no new categories invented by the model.

02

Route

High-confidence tickets go straight to the right queue. Nothing skips a human when confidence is low.

03

Escalate

Anything flagged urgent or low-confidence pages the on-call agent directly, through the same bot covered on page 11.

04

Learn

Every human override is logged as a training signal — the model that ships in Q3 is trained partly on its own Q2 mistakes.

04 — Model & Method

Fine-tuned, not built from scratch.

A pretrained encoder plus a small trained head beats a bespoke model here — the taxonomy is stable and the label volume is modest. Train from scratch only when a pretrained base genuinely can't represent the problem.

Base modelOpen-weight sentence encoder
Fine-tuning methodLinear classification head, full-parameter frozen
Context window512 tokens
Serving frameworkManaged inference endpoint, autoscaled
VersioningModel registry, immutable tags per release

Why this shape

A frozen encoder means retraining is cheap — the head is the only thing that moves when the taxonomy shifts or new labeled overrides arrive. That trade-off matters more here than squeezing out the last point of accuracy a full fine-tune might buy.

08 — Automation & Monitoring

The system watches itself.

Drift detection

Input embeddings are compared weekly against the training distribution. A shift past threshold opens a ticket of its own — on the team, not a customer's queue.

Alerting

Latency, error rate, and override rate page on-call directly — the same escalation bot shown on page 11, not a separate tool.

Auto-retrain

Once 2,000 fresh overrides accumulate, a retraining job queues automatically — it still ships through the same eval gate as every other release.
